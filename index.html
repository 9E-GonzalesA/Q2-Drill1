Beginner's Guide to Badminton

Basic Rules


  • First to 21 points wins the game.
  • You can only hit the shuttlecock once when it is in your side.
  • You lose the point if the shuttlecock goes out the court.

Badminton Equipment


  • Badminton racket.
  • shuttlecock
  • Net or court.

Basic Techniques


  • Grips (foregrip and backhand grip).
  • Serving (backhand and forehand).
  • Hitting styles (smash and faint).
